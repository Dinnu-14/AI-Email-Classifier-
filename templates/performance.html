Machine Learning Methodology Explained
1. Text Preprocessing

Raw emails undergo lowercasing to avoid casing disparity (e.g. URGENT vs urgent), followed by whitespace normalization. This preserves semantic vocabulary while filtering noise.

2. TF-IDF Vectorizer

Translates words into statistical importance scores: Term Frequency (how often word appears in the email) multiplied by Inverse Document Frequency (rarity across all emails). English stop-words are eliminated and unigrams + bigrams are captured.

3. Multinomial Naive Bayes

Computes the posterior probability $P(Category | Words)$ using Bayes' Theorem. Laplace smoothing ($\alpha = 0.5$) prevents zero-probability issues for unseen words. Fast, highly effective, and completely transparent for text classification.
